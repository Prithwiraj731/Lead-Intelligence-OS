================================================================================
 LEAD INTELLIGENCE OS - GMAIL SETUP & LIVE EMAIL OUTREACH GUIDE
================================================================================

This guide walks you through every single step required to configure real Gmail
sending so that after you research leads and edit email drafts, you can dispatch
actual emails through your Gmail account.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1: GOOGLE CLOUD CONSOLE SETUP (OAUTH 2.0 CREDENTIALS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To send emails directly through your personal or Google Workspace Gmail account,
you need an OAuth 2.0 Client ID and Secret from Google Cloud.

Step 1: Create a Google Cloud Project
  1. Open https://console.cloud.google.com/ in your browser.
  2. Sign in with the Google / Gmail account you want to send emails from.
  3. Click the Project dropdown at the top and click "NEW PROJECT".
  4. Project Name: "Lead Intelligence OS" (or any name you prefer).
  5. Click "CREATE" and select the newly created project.

Step 2: Enable the Gmail API
  1. In the search bar at the top, type "Gmail API".
  2. Click on "Gmail API" from the marketplace/services list.
  3. Click "ENABLE".

Step 3: Configure the OAuth Consent Screen
  1. In the left navigation menu, go to "APIs & Services" > "OAuth consent screen".
  2. User Type: Select "External" and click "CREATE".
  3. App information:
     - App name: "Lead Intelligence OS"
     - User support email: select your email address.
     - Developer contact email: enter your email address.
  4. Click "SAVE AND CONTINUE".
  5. Scopes:
     - Click "ADD OR REMOVE SCOPES".
     - In the filter search bar, find:
         https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send
         https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email
     - Check both checkboxes and click "UPDATE".
     - Click "SAVE AND CONTINUE".
  6. Test users:
     - Click "+ ADD USERS".
     - Enter your Gmail address (the one you are sending from).
       (Important: In "Testing" mode, only added test users can authenticate).
     - Click "ADD", then click "SAVE AND CONTINUE".
  7. Click "BACK TO DASHBOARD".

Step 4: Create OAuth 2.0 Web Client Credentials
  1. In the left navigation menu, click "Credentials".
  2. Click "+ CREATE CREDENTIALS" at the top and select "OAuth client ID".
  3. Application type: Select "Web application".
  4. Name: "Lead Intelligence Web App".
  5. Authorized redirect URIs:
     - Click "+ ADD URI".
     - Enter EXACTLY:
         http://localhost:3000/api/auth/gmail/callback
  6. Click "CREATE".
  7. A modal will pop up displaying:
     - Your Client ID (looks like: xxx.apps.googleusercontent.com)
     - Your Client Secret (looks like: GOCSPX-xxx)
  8. Copy both values.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2: CONFIGURE THE .env FILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open the ".env" file in your project root:
c:\Users\USER\Desktop\LEAD INTELLIGENCE OS\.env

Add / update the following lines:

# -------------------------------------------------------------
# OUTBOUND LIVE EMAIL CONFIGURATION
# -------------------------------------------------------------
OUTBOUND_DELIVERY_ENABLED="true"
OUTBOUND_DRY_RUN="false"
EMAIL_PROVIDER="gmail"

# -------------------------------------------------------------
# GMAIL OAUTH CREDENTIALS (from Google Cloud Console)
# -------------------------------------------------------------
GMAIL_CLIENT_ID="YOUR_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID.apps.googleusercontent.com"
GMAIL_CLIENT_SECRET="YOUR_GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET"
GMAIL_REDIRECT_URI="http://localhost:3000/api/auth/gmail/callback"

Save the .env file.
If your dev server is running, restart it so the new environment variables load:
In terminal: press Ctrl+C, then run:
  pnpm dev

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3: CONNECT YOUR GMAIL ACCOUNT IN THE UI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Open your browser and go to:
   http://localhost:3000/pilot
   (or http://localhost:3000/settings)

2. Look at the "Gmail Account (OAuth 2.0)" card in the sidebar or top banner.
   - It will currently show: "Gmail Disconnected"

3. Click the button: "Connect Gmail".
   - You will be redirected to Google's OAuth consent screen.
   - Sign in to your Gmail account.
   - You may see a screen saying "Google hasn't verified this app" (since your app is in testing mode). Click "Advanced", then click "Go to Lead Intelligence OS (unsafe)".
   - Check the permission: "Send email on your behalf".
   - Click "Continue".

4. Google redirects you back to:
   http://localhost:3000/pilot
   - The card will now show:
     "Connected: your_email@gmail.com" with a green shield/check icon.
   - The tokens are stored locally on the server in "scratch/.gmail-oauth.json" and refreshed automatically.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 4: THE END-TO-END WORKFLOW (IMPORT -> AUDIT -> EDIT -> APPROVE -> SEND)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: Ingest Leads with Contact Email Addresses
  1. Go to: http://localhost:3000/leads/import
  2. Upload your CSV or XLSX file (or use sample_data/dubai_leads.csv).
  3. Ensure each row has:
     - Company Name
     - Contact Name
     - Contact Email (e.g., target@company.com)
     - Website / Domain
  4. Complete import.
  NOTE: The safety gate strictly blocks disposable/temporary emails (mailinator, 10minutemail, etc.).

STEP 2: Run Research & Technical Opportunity Audit
  1. Go to: http://localhost:3000/leads
  2. Click on the lead you want to target.
  3. Click "Run Research".
  4. The engine performs:
     - Deep website crawl (inspecting mobile responsiveness, SSL, CMS, conversion forms).
     - AI / Rule Engine analysis calculating Opportunity Score and Confidence Score.
     - Verification that the lead is eligible for outreach (Decision = "PROCEED").

STEP 3: Generate, Review & Edit the Email Draft
  1. Go to: http://localhost:3000/messages
  2. Locate the message for that company (initial status is "READY_FOR_REVIEW").
  3. Click into the message to review:
     - Pitch angle & verified technical evidence
     - Subject line
     - Email body
  4. Edit Draft:
     - Click "Edit"
     - Customize the email body or subject line to your liking
     - Click "Save Changes"

STEP 4: Approve the Message (Mandatory Safety Gate)
  1. On the message details page or list, click "Approve".
  2. Status changes to "APPROVED".
  CRITICAL: Lead Intelligence OS has a 7-layer safety gate. Unapproved messages CANNOT be sent under any circumstances.

STEP 5: Dispatch the Email Actually via Gmail
  There are two ways to dispatch the approved email:

  METHOD A: Via Campaign Pilot Console (Recommended)
    1. Navigate to: http://localhost:3000/pilot
    2. Under "Eligible Pilot Prospects", find your approved lead.
    3. You will see a mandatory confirmation checkbox:
       [ ] "I have reviewed this recipient, evidence, and message"
    4. Check this checkbox.
    5. Click "Dispatch Safe Email".
    6. The system executes:
       - Suppression check (ensure email is not unsubscribed)
       - 14-day duplicate outreach prevention
       - Rate limit check (< 20/hr, < 100/day)
       - Google Gmail API: POST https://gmail.googleapis.com/gmail/v1/users/me/messages/send
    7. A green delivery receipt modal will appear with:
       - Status: SENT
       - Provider: GMAIL_OAUTH
       - Provider Message ID: Google's unique message ID
    8. Check your real Gmail account's "Sent" folder — the email will be there!

  METHOD B: Via Messages Queue API
    You can trigger delivery directly via POST request:
      POST http://localhost:3000/api/messages/<MESSAGE_ID>/deliver
      Content-Type: application/json
      {
        "channel": "EMAIL",
        "forceDryRun": false
      }

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 5: SAFETY RULES & LIMITS SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Pilot Hard Cap:
   By default, the pilot campaign limits live sending to 5 real recipients
   (safety guard against accidental mass blasts during onboarding).
   To increase this limit, check "PILOT_MAX_REAL_RECIPIENTS" in:
   apps/web/lib/delivery/safety-gate.ts

2. Rate Limits:
   - Maximum 20 emails per hour.
   - Maximum 100 emails per 24 hours.

3. Duplicate Suppression:
   - If an email was already sent to this company in the last 14 days,
     the safety gate blocks it with "DUPLICATE_OUTREACH_DETECTED".

4. Idempotency Key:
   - Every delivery generates a unique SHA-256 hash (messageId + recipient + channel).
   - If you click send twice, the system returns the existing delivery receipt
     without re-sending the email.
